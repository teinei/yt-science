### Simple Explanation (Grade 3 level):

In the 1960s, a scientist named **John Calhoun** wanted to see what would happen if mice had a perfect world.

He built a big "mouse paradise" called **Universe 25**. It had:
- Lots of food and water
- Warm and safe places to live
- No predators (no cats or owls to eat them)
- Plenty of space at first

He started with just 8 healthy mice.

At first, the mice were very happy. They had babies, and the population grew quickly — up to **2,200 mice**!

But then strange things started to happen:

- Some mice stopped fighting for food or space.
- Many stopped having babies.
- Some mice became very aggressive.
- Others became very shy and hid all the time.
- Mothers stopped taking care of their babies.
- The mice stopped acting normal.

Even though there was still enough food, the mouse society **broke down**. Most of the mice died, and the whole group almost disappeared.

### The Big Idea:
The scientist learned that even when animals (or people) have everything they need — food, safety, and a home — they still need **space, purpose, and normal social life**. Too many creatures living too close together can make things fall apart.

This experiment is often talked about when people think about what happens in very crowded cities or when societies become too comfortable.
